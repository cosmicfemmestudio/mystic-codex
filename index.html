Questions

What is The Modern Mystic's Codex?

A crystal collection and apothecary app that runs in your browser. Keep track of your crystals, herbs, resins, oils and candles, look up their meanings in a built-in library, and follow the moon and the Wheel of the Year in your practice.

What is in the library?

50 crystals with their meanings, chakras, elements and care (including which stones must stay dry or out of the sun), 40 herbs and resins, 14 essential oils, 12 candle colors, the 8 moon phases and the magical days of the week.

How does it help me care for my crystals?

It records when each crystal was last cleansed and charged, reminds you around each full and new moon, and lets you mark your whole collection as charged in one tap.

Does it follow the Wheel of the Year?

Yes. It shows all eight sabbats (Samhain, Yule, Imbolc, Ostara, Beltane, Litha, Lughnasadh and Mabon) on the right dates for the northern or southern hemisphere, with altar ideas, crystals, herbs and a simple ritual for each.

Does it include safety information?

Yes. Herbs, resins and oils carry safety notes, such as which are toxic to pets or should be avoided in pregnancy, oils carry dilution warnings, and every page reminds you never to leave candles or burning herbs unattended.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your collection and photos stay on your own device, and you can download a backup file at any time.

Does it work offline?

The full Codex installs on your Home Screen like an app and works with no internet connection.

What is the difference between the demo and the full Codex?

The free demo holds 5 crystals, 5 apothecary supplies and 3 photos, and opens part of the library. The full Codex has no limits, opens the whole library and all eight sabbats, and works offline.
